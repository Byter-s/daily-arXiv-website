[1] Li$^{2}$SAO: Lifting Light Singl-Rollout Asynchronous RL Optimization via Group-Relative Actor-Critic Bootstrapping cs.CL | cs.LGPDF

Zichen Bai, Yanli Wang, Yang Liu, Junhao Shi, Xueqin Liu

TL;DR: 论文研究异步强化学习用于LLM后训练时，SAO依赖闭源预训练value model的问题，并提出轻量方案Li^2SAO。作者先复现无预训练value model的LiSAO，发现GRPO在单轮推理任务更好，而LiSAO在多轮agentic任务更好，但LiSAO因critic冷启动在早期训练明显落后。Li^2SAO在critic冷启动阶段用group-relative advantage训练actor、用group-centered return训练critic，之后切换到single-rollout GAE，从而兼顾早期稳定性和后期性能上限。

Details

Motivation: 异步RL能提升LLM后训练效率，但SAO依赖闭源的、经scaling value pretraining得到的value model，轻量复现的LiSAO因value model/critic未训练好而在早期训练落后于GRPO。论文旨在消除该依赖并解决critic冷启动问题。

Result: 实验覆盖单轮推理和多轮agentic任务；摘要未给出具体数值或benchmark名称，仅定性表明Li^2SAO在早期训练中赶上或超过GRPO，并进一步提升后期性能上限。

Insight: 创新点是group-relative actor-critic bootstrapping：critic冷启动时用group-relative advantage训练actor、group-centered return训练critic，待critic可用后切换到single-rollout GAE。客观看，其价值在于结合GRPO的group相对优势与SAO的单rollout异步优化，缓解异步RL中value model冷启动和闭源依赖问题。

Abstract: Asynchronous reinforcement learning (RL) is becoming a mainstream paradigm for post-training large language models (LLMs), as it improves training efficiency by updating the model as rollouts arrive. Recent work argues that group-wise sampling in GRPO does not naturally fit asynchronous RL training because rollout lengths follow a long-tailed distribution, and proposes Single-rollout Asynchronous Optimization (SAO) to improve stability and off-policy behavior, especially on agentic tasks. However, SAO relies on a closed-source value model obtained from scaling value pretraining. In this paper, we analyze GRPO and LiSAO, our lightweight reproduction of SAO without the pretrained value model, on single-turn reasoning and multi-turn agentic tasks. We find that GRPO outperforms LiSAO on reasoning tasks, while the reverse holds on agentic tasks. Since the value model of LiSAO is not yet well trained , LiSAO lags behind GRPO substantially in early training steps. To address this issue, we propose Li$^{2}$SAO, which Lifts Light Single-rollout Asynchronous Optimization with group-relative actor-critic bootstrapping: while the critic is cold, a group-relative advantage trains the actor and a group-centered return trains the critic, after which training switches to single-rollout GAE. Experiments show that Li$^{2}$SAO catches up with or surpasses GRPO in early training and further raises the later performance ceiling. Code is available at https://github.com/jd-cc-coder/Li2SAO.

[3] CoFiBreak: A Coarse-to-Fine Diversity Reward for Effective Multi-Turn Jailbreak cs.CLPDF

TL;DR: 论文提出 CoFiBreak，一种用于 GRPO 训练的由粗到细多样性奖励机制，用于自动化多轮越狱红队测试。粗粒度奖励鼓励攻击者使用 GRPO 组内欠代表的攻击策略，细粒度奖励在移除有害意图信息后惩罚重复的人设、框架和输出格式，从而同时提升策略层和实现层的攻击多样性，并保持攻击成功率与推理效率。

Details

Motivation: 仅以攻击成功为唯一奖励的强化学习会让攻击者 LLM 收敛到狭窄的提示集合，导致攻击多样性崩塌，使安全评估反复测试近重复的攻击模式而非真实有害意图；现有方法往往需要以攻击成功率或推理成本为代价换取多样性。

Result: 以 Llama3.1-8B-Instruct 为受害者训练，在 HarmBench 上发现的独特成功细粒度策略数是最强基线的 1.6-2.0 倍；在 AdvBench 上达到 98.8% ASR@10，多样性分数为所有对比方法中最高，是最强攻击基线的 2.1 倍；迁移到未见过的 Claude-Opus-4.6 仍达 71.0% ASR@10。

Insight: 创新点在于将多样性奖励拆分为粗（策略选择）与细（策略实现）两级，并仅对已超过有效性阈值的攻击施加多样性奖励，避免以无效攻击换取多样性；同时奖励只在训练阶段计算，推理时无额外开销。

Such a collapse of attack diversity can bias safety evaluation toward repeatedly testing near-duplicate attack patterns rather than the underlying harmful intents. Existing methods often trade off attack success or inference cost for greater content or strategy diversity. We propose CoFiBreak, a coarse-to-fine diversity reward for Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO) that improves attack diversity while preserving strong attack effectiveness and low inference cost. The coarse reward broadens which strategies the attacker uses by encouraging strategies underrepresented within each GRPO group, while the fine reward broadens how those strategies are realized by discouraging reuse of the same personas, framings, and output formats after harmful-intent information is removed from prompt embeddings. Diversity rewards apply only to attacks that already exceed an effectiveness threshold, so diversity cannot be gained through ineffective attacks. Since both rewards are computed only during training, CoFiBreak also retains inference efficiency. Trained with Llama3.1-8B-Instruct as the victim, CoFiBreak discovers 1.6-2.0x as many distinct successful fine-grained strategies as the strongest baseline across frontier victims on HarmBench. It reaches 98.8% ASR@10 (attack success rate with 10 generations per intent) on AdvBench with the highest diversity score among all compared methods, which is 2.1x that of the strongest-attacking baseline. The resulting attacks transfer to unseen frontier LLMs, reaching 71.0% ASR@10 on AdvBench with the unseen victim Claude-Opus-4.6. The code will be released.

Abstract: HER2 status decides who receives HER2-targeted therapy. Pathologists score HER2 protein by immunohistochemistry (IHC); equivocal IHC 2+ tumours, their hardest calls, wait for in situ hybridisation (ISH). We built a HER2 reader for routine H&E slides, owned end to end, as a baseline for pathologists. A 22M-parameter ViT-S/16 tile encoder was trained on public TCGA slides by self-supervision, distillation from H-optimus-0 and two rounds of JEPA refinement; five cross-validated attention-based slide classifiers average their probabilities; and an operating point is fixed on training data. Compared under one paired ten-seed protocol, readers on our encoder rank first of 7 on external validation from TCGA-BRCA to Yale-HER2 (AUC 0.812), level with readers on encoders 4 and 50 times larger. We also rebuilt PathBench’s encoder benchmark on public cohorts; our replica reproduces its published order of reference encoders, and under it our encoder ranks first of 7 on TCGA-BRCA (0.689) and sits level with H-optimus-0 and Phikon on Yale-HER2 (0.897). On three external hospitals our deployed reader reaches AUC 0.82 on Yale-HER2 (about 0.81 with training slides balanced for tissue size), 0.75 on HEROHE and 0.63 on BCNB biopsies, and among HEROHE’s 85 IHC 2+ cases it ranks ISH-amplified above non-amplified tumours at 0.76, best among our TCGA-trained readers. Per-laboratory score normalisation and a laboratory’s own slides improve it further on a new site. Without a GPU our reader needs 5-6 s per slide. It serves as a research-use pre-read in a pathology platform, where it proposes a probability and a call beside IHC and helps prioritise ISH for equivocal cases. Weights, per-slide predictions and the data behind every figure are public at https://huggingface.co/cloudkites/her2-reader.
