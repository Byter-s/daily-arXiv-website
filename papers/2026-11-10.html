TL;DR: 论文提出 RL-ATTACK，一个基于强化学习的可训练黑盒对抗攻击框架，通过从目标语言模型获取反馈来生成定制化对抗变换，从而攻击 LLM。方法在自然语言推理和生成式问答任务上评估，强调更低攻击后准确率、更高查询效率和更好语义保持。

Details

Motivation: 现有黑盒对抗攻击在无法获取梯度时，往往依赖外部知识猜测对抗变换，忽略了目标模型本身提供的信息。论文希望利用目标模型反馈学习更有效的定制化攻击。

Abstract: Large language models (LLMs) exhibit vulnerabilities to adversarial perturbations that can induce unintended responses. These vulnerabilities can be identified via intentional attacks and subsequently used to improve model safety. In black-box settings where gradients are inaccessible, existing methods tend to guess adversarial transformations using external knowledge, ignoring the information provided by the target model. To mitigate this, we propose a trainable black-box framework based on reinforcement learning, which learns from the target model to generate tailored adversarial transformations. Abstract: Training smaller language models on reasoning solutions generated by a larger teacher model is the prevailing approach for transferring mathematical reasoning capabilities. However, the student tends to overfit the output distribution of the teacher and generalizes poorly when its own behavior at inference time deviates from the training data. Recent methods attempt to bridge this gap by incorporating error data, yet the corrective trajectories still reflect the reasoning patterns of the teacher, which the student cannot reliably reproduce, leaving the distribution mismatch largely unresolved. We propose a method, Failure Profiling and Reachable Trajectory Selection (FPRT), built on the insight that effective training data should be jointly determined by what the student fails on and what it can realistically learn. Our method first profiles the failure behavior of the student on each problem through repeated sampling, characterizing it in terms of error rate and error consistency to reveal qualitatively distinct failure modes. These first corrective steps constitute the primary bottleneck, while the remaining steps follow an already corrected context and can therefore be continued more readily. This contrasts with standard reranking approaches, which score the trajectory as a whole and thereby dilute the signal at the critical reasoning transition. Experiments demonstrate that our method achieves the SOTA performance across the five benchmarks on both student backbones.
